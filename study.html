Curriculum

The curriculum describes the courses, credits and learning objectives of a degree programme. It helps students plan their studies.

Study Policy

Study policies explain the rules and responsibilities related to studying, assessment and completing courses.

Thesis

A thesis is an independent project completed towards the end of a degree programme. It demonstrates the student's knowledge and skills.

Practical Training

Practical training allows students to gain professional experience and apply their knowledge in a real working environment.

Exchange Studies

Exchange studies provide opportunities to study abroad and develop international experience.

Credit Transfer

Credit transfer allows students to apply for recognition of relevant studies completed previously. Applications can be made through the appropriate study administration services.

Cross-Institutional Studies

Cross-institutional studies allow students to take eligible courses offered by other higher education institutions.
